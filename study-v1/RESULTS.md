# Study v1: saturation finding

**Both independent adaptation and policy-assisted adaptation passed all six
required fixtures on their first attempts. No measured success-rate gain was
observed. This workload is too easy to support the intended formal task.**

The prospectively fixed experiment used one canonical cycling-pelican asset,
three development briefs, one method-writing call and one paired transfer
comparison. Transfer generation budgets were matched: Astra low, two maximum
calls each, 180 seconds per call, zero automatic retries. The instruction method
was frozen before either transfer condition and contained development evidence
only. Transfer prompts differed only by that method. Method-development compute
was additional and is reported separately.

| Condition | First attempt | Second attempt | Selected | Required fixtures passed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Independent adaptation | Target pass; 5/5 retention | Target pass; 5/5 retention | First | 6/6 |
| Policy-assisted adaptation | Target pass; 5/5 retention | Target pass; 5/5 retention | First | 6/6 |

All three development outputs passed their own target and both prior fixtures.
Developer-authored controls verified feasibility, including a 2,458-triangle
asset satisfying all six fixtures. Removing animation or the docking marker
produced the expected failures. These controls are never model scores.

Eight model calls completed: three development asset calls, one method call,
and four transfer asset calls. Eight distinct original native sessions and
257 hashed record files were verified. Generated asset sources and the learned
policy match the raw model responses without human edits. Full native records,
prompts, responses, failed outcomes and execution logs remain local; the public
package includes the frozen protocol, exact evaluated assets/sources, method,
outcomes and offline reproduction fixtures. See
[audited summary](../reports/study-v1-summary.json) and
[pre-call freeze](../reports/study-v1-freeze.json).

## Why this is not yet an adequate research benchmark

The new fixture only tightens ceiling, width and triangle limits while keeping
the same docking interface. Its requirements subsume those of the retention
fixtures. Consequently, passing the compound fixture largely implies passing
the earlier ones. The six fixtures are overlapping requirements, not six
independent demonstrations of continual adaptation or forgetting resistance.

The candidate also receives complete canonical construction source. Under these
conditions, a shared static geometry change can solve every scene. The current
part-volume and motion checks do not fully measure visual identity or prevent
every unrealistic deformation. One asset and one paired comparison cannot
establish transfer across asset families or a stable causal method advantage.

No thresholds, budgets, fixtures or selection rules were changed after observing
model results. This v1 remains an archived calibration result; it is not an
official reward, strong-model baseline, Harbor validation or completed RSI run.
Running it longer would not establish missing score headroom.

## Requirements for a separately versioned next study

- Introduce genuinely different scene demands, such as an upright interaction
  pose and a crouched clearance pose, with controlled per-scene adapters.
- Define what geometry and asset features must remain unchanged, beyond simple
  minimum bounding volumes; make permitted changes explicit.
- Test the reusable method on more than one asset and new scene combinations.
- Validate feasible reference adapters and broken-adapter controls before model
  calls; freeze the new workload and score separately.
- Calibrate the intended formal model configuration before a long run or
  claiming formal submission readiness.

These are next-study design requirements, not results already implemented or
contributor-confirmed formal reward choices.
